# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want TaskSeam to be a respectful, constructive project. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept correction, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owners through GitHub. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical.
